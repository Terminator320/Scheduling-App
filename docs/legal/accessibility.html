ES Pro Accessibility

Scheduling app for managing appointments, clients, and employees · by Plombier Eau Secours
ES Pro for iPhone, version 1.46 · Last updated August 14, 2026

ES Pro is a working tool. It gets used on a phone, outdoors, in a van, in the dark, with gloves on and one hand free. If it can't be read or heard, it can't be used. So this isn't a box we tick; it's the app doing its job.

Here's what works today, what doesn't yet, and how to tell us when we've got it wrong. All of it is on for everyone: there's no separate “accessible mode,” and nothing here costs extra.

What works

The accessibility features Apple lists on the App Store, and where ES Pro stands on each.
FeatureWhere it standsWhat that means in the app
VoiceOver Works Every button, row, and field says what it is, in English and in French. A job reads as one sentence (who it's for, when, which crew, what state it's in) instead of a dozen fragments you have to piece back together. A day on the calendar tells you its date and how many jobs are on it, because the little coloured dots can't be read out loud. Logos and decoration stay quiet.
Voice Control Works Say “tap Save” and it taps Save. The names Voice Control uses are the same ones VoiceOver reads, and “show numbers” works throughout. Buttons and rows are big enough to hit with gloves on.
Bigger text Works ES Pro follows your iPhone's Larger Text setting. It also has its own size setting (Small, Medium, Large, XL) for when you want this app bigger without making every other app bigger too. The two stack, up to about twice normal size. Screens reflow to fit rather than cutting words off.
Dark appearance Works Follows your iPhone, or you can pin it to light or dark in the app if you'd rather decide yourself. Dark isn't light mode turned inside out; it's drawn on purpose.
Not colour alone Works Colour is never the only thing telling you something. A job's state is a badge that says “Scheduled” or “Complete.” Crew colours sit right next to the crew's names. Losing signal says “offline” in words. The one colour-only marking, the crew dots on the month calendar, gets read out as a number of jobs instead.
Contrast Works Text and controls are dark enough on light and light enough on dark to stay readable in a bright driveway or a dim basement. We hold them to the usual web standard for contrast (WCAG AA). Faded grey text is only ever used for extras, never for something you can't find spelled out somewhere else.
Reduce Motion Works Turn on Reduce Motion and the app stops moving: no sliding screens, no swinging calendar months, no shimmer while things load, no animated charts, no drifting photos. Things just appear.
Captions Nothing to caption There's no video and no sound in ES Pro. Job photos are still pictures.
Audio descriptions Nothing to describe Same story: no video anywhere in the app.

Settings inside ES Pro

Open Settings from the menu:

  • Text size. Small, Medium, Large, or XL, with a preview so you can see it before you commit. It changes the whole app, and it sits on top of whatever your iPhone is already doing.
  • Appearance. Follow your phone, or force light or dark.
  • Language. English or French, whichever your phone is set to. What VoiceOver reads, what notifications say, and what Siri tells you all follow your choice.
  • Notifications. A shortcut straight to your iPhone's notification settings for ES Pro, for when reminders stop arriving.
  • Replay app tour. Runs the walkthrough of each screen again, as many times as you like.

Settings on your iPhone

These live on the phone, not in ES Pro. Turn one on and the app picks it up straight away.

  • VoiceOver: Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver
  • Voice Control: Settings → Accessibility → Voice Control
  • Larger Text: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text
  • Bold Text: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Bold Text
  • Increase Contrast: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Increase Contrast
  • Reduce Motion: Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion
  • Dark appearance: Settings → Display & Brightness → Dark

Where we fall short

We'd rather tell you here than have you find out on a job at four in the afternoon.

  • The crew map. The map itself is Google's, and VoiceOver can't reach the individual pins on it. Everything shown there is also in the staff roster. Open that from the map and you get the same people as a plain list: name, how recent their location is, how far away they are, closest first.
  • The weekly chart. On the dashboard it reads as a summary rather than bar by bar. The same numbers are written out beside it.
  • Status badges stop growing. Past about 1.3× they hold their size so “In progress” stays on one line instead of breaking apart. Everything else keeps getting bigger.
  • Job photos have no description. VoiceOver will say there's an image, but not what's in it. They're snapped on site by whoever's there, and nobody stops to write a caption.
  • We haven't checked every corner. We test the paths people walk every day (signing in, reading the schedule, opening a job, marking it done) with VoiceOver on, Reduce Motion on, and the text turned up. Past that we're relying on you. If a screen doesn't work for you, that's a bug, not a feature request, and we'll treat it like one.

Tell us what's in your way

If any part of ES Pro is hard or impossible to use, write to george@vogas.net and put Accessibility in the subject. We answer within 2 business days.

It helps if you can say what you were trying to do, where you were in the app, what you use to get around (VoiceOver, bigger text, something else), and which iPhone and which version of iOS.

We'll tell you straight whether we can reproduce it, whether we're fixing it, and we'll come back to you when the fix ships.
